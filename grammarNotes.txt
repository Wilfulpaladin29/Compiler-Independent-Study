Def of Grammars: 

- A set of terminal symbols (tokens)
- non-terminals (syntactic variables) is a set of strings of terminals
- Productions, a production consists of a nonterminal, called the head or
head or left side, a production is basically a culmination of a non-terminal on the
left side and on the right side has other nonterminals or terminals
- A designation of one of the nonterminals as the start syumbol
